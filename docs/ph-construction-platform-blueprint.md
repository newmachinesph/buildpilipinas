# BuildRight PH — Platform Blueprint
*Working title. The complete platform for building anything in the Philippines: learn, plan, compute, buy, hire, finance.*

**Vision:** From house plan to move-in — one platform.
**Positioning:** Wikipedia + calculator + Lazada + escrow for Philippine construction.
**Initial market:** One province/region. Small contractors + owner/house-builders.

---

## 1. The Six Pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Why it wins |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Knowledge Library | How-to-build-right guides (Taglish) | Free traffic engine, trust builder |
| 2. Plans & Estimators | House plans + cost calculators + BOQ tools | Converts browsers to buyers |
| 3. Marketplace | Materials, hardware, aggregates, equipment | Core transaction revenue |
| 4. Services Network | Haulers, testers, engineers, trades | Higher margins, stickiness |
| 5. Trust Layer | Escrow, contracts, inspections, warranty | The moat nobody in PH has |
| 6. Financing | Materials credit, loan guidance, referrals | Biggest long-term revenue |

## 2. Pillar Detail

### Pillar 1 — Knowledge Library ("Build It Right")
Content categories:
- **Build journey guides:** site prep → foundation → structure → roofing → MEPF → finishing; what happens, how long, what it costs per stage
- **Quality-check guides ("Tamang Gawa"):** rebar spacing, concrete curing, CHB laying, mortar ratios, roofing fastening — with photos of right vs wrong
- **Materials 101:** cement types/grades, CHB per sqm, rebar computation, plywood grades, paint coverage
- **Permits & legal:** building permit checklist per LGU type, occupancy permit, electrical permit, titling basics
- **Disaster-resilient building:** typhoon-proof roofing details, seismic best practices, flood-zone elevation guidance
- **Financing education:** Pag-IBIG housing loan steps, bank construction loans, in-house financing traps
- Formats: articles (SEO), YouTube/TikTok shorts (reach), downloadable checklists (lead capture)

### Pillar 2 — Plans, Calculators & Estimators
**House plans library:**
- Filterable catalog: lot size, budget, bedrooms, storeys, style (bungalow, 2-storey, roofdeck, tiny home, apartment/rental unit)
- Each plan ships with: floor plan, elevations, estimated bill of materials (BOM), cost range for the user's region, buildable-on-your-lot checker
- Free tier (browse + basic BOM) / paid tier (full signed plans, permit-ready set)

**Calculator suite (each one is an SEO landing page + lead magnet):**
- Total build cost estimator (₱/sqm by finish level: basic, standard, premium)
- Concrete volume + cement/sand/gravel bags calculator (slab, footing, column)
- CHB / wall materials calculator
- Rebar/steel weight calculator
- Roofing area + sheets calculator
- Paint coverage calculator
- Tile quantity calculator
- Electrical load estimator
- Septic tank sizing
- Labor cost estimator (per trade, per region)
- Loan amortization calculator (Pag-IBIG/bank rates)
- Solar sizing + payback calculator (kWh usage → system size → savings)

**The killer flow:** Calculator result → auto-generated materials list → "Get 3 quotes from suppliers near you" → order. Every calculator is a funnel into the marketplace.

**BOQ upload tool (for contractors):** upload bill of quantities → platform returns 3 supplier quotes within 24h. This is the contractor hook.

### Pillar 3 — Marketplace
**Sellers (launch):** neighborhood hardware stores, cement/rebar/CHB dealers, sand & gravel haulers, lumber/steel yards
**Sellers (year 1–2):** ready-mix plants, electrical/plumbing/tile/paint specialty shops, equipment rental (mixers, scaffolding, backhoes), solar suppliers
**Sellers (scale):** manufacturers/brands direct, private label (Infra.Market playbook)

**Buyers (launch):** small contractors (₱500K–₱5M projects), owner-builders and their maestros
**Buyers (later):** mid-size firms, developers; avoid DIY-retail and government early

**Mechanics:** request-for-quote first (respects supplier price sensitivity), open price lists later; commission 2–3% commodities, 5–8% finishing/specialty; delivery margin per truck trip; bulk-pooling spread on aggregated small orders

### Pillar 4 — Services Network
- **Tier 1 (launch):** hauling/delivery, equipment rental with operator, concrete pumping, demolition, soil testing/boring
- **Tier 2 (after trust systems):** masonry, carpentry, tile, painting, electrical, plumbing, welding, roofing, waterproofing crews
- **Tier 3 (early — easy to vet via PRC license):** structural/signing engineers, architects, geodetic surveyors, permit processing, construction supervision
- **Tier 4 (scale):** vetted general contractors for full design-and-build packages
- **Green/energy services:** solar PV installation packages, solar water heaters, rainwater harvesting — bundled as add-ons at the finishing stage

### Pillar 5 — Trust Layer (the moat)
- **Escrow milestone payments:** owner funds held, released per completed + inspected stage (Brick&Bolt model)
- **Standard contracts:** plain-language Taglish construction agreements, downloadable free (trust builder) with premium templates
- **Inspection service:** trained inspectors (CE grads) verify milestones with photo checklists; per-visit fee
- **Ratings & verified reviews** for suppliers, crews, professionals
- **Dispute mediation** process
- **Workmanship warranty** program at scale

### Pillar 6 — Financing
- **Phase A (referral only):** partner with banks/lenders for construction loans and Pag-IBIG guidance; earn referral fees (1–2%)
- **Phase B:** purchase-order financing for contractors via lending partner, underwritten with platform transaction history
- **Phase C:** materials installment for owner-builders ("build now, pay per stage"), supplier early-payout factoring
- Never lend from own balance sheet early.

## 4. Roadmap

**Phase 0 — Content & audience (months 0–4, ~₱0 capital)**
- Launch site + FB/TikTok/YouTube with 20 quality-check guides, 10 calculators, 15 free house plans w/ BOMs
- Target: 10K monthly visitors, 2K followers, email/Messenger list
- Meanwhile: interview 20–30 contractors, 10–15 suppliers in chosen province

**Phase 1 — Manual marketplace (months 3–8)**
- BOQ-to-3-quotes via Messenger/Viber; broker orders at 2–3% + delivery margin
- Sign 20–30 suppliers personally; serve 20–50 active contractor/owner buyers
- Target: first ₱1M monthly GMV brokered manually

**Phase 2 — Platform v1 (months 8–14)**
- Web app: calculators → materials list → RFQ → quotes → order tracking; supplier dashboard
- Add Tier 1 services + engineer/surveyor directory
- Target: ₱5–10M monthly GMV, 50–80 active contractors, 40+ suppliers in one province

**Phase 3 — Trust + expansion (months 14–24)**
- Escrow pilots, inspection service, paid plans, adjacent-province expansion
- Financing partner signed; solar packages piloted

**Phase 4 — Scale (year 2–3)**
- Regional expansion, trades marketplace, PO financing, possible private label

## 7. Key Metrics
- Phase 0: monthly visitors, calculator uses, list signups
- Phase 1+: GMV, take rate, active buyers, repeat order rate (the metric), supplier count in-province, quote turnaround time

## 8. Top Risks & Mitigations
1. **Disintermediation** (buyer + supplier bypass you) → win on delivery, credit terms, and pooled pricing they can't get alone
2. **Cash flow from credit expectations** → never extend own credit; COD/GCash first, financing partners later
3. **BuildHub expands into your province** → density + relationships + content moat; be locally 10x better
4. **Supplier price-listing resistance** → RFQ model first, not public catalogs
5. **Service quality disputes** → delay trades marketplace until inspection + ratings systems exist
6. **Student bandwidth** → phase 0 content is doable around classes; raise small grant/competition funding (DTI, DOST-TAPI, university incubators, PSE/startup competitions) before phase 2

## 9. Competitive Positioning (one line each)
- **vs BuildHub.ph:** they sell products nationally and thinly; we own one region deeply with content, services, and trust
- **vs Shoppable Business:** they do generic B2B procurement; we are construction-native (BOQ, plans, engineering content)
- **vs Facebook/suki status quo:** we don't replace relationships; we add quotes, delivery, escrow, and knowledge on top

**North star:** the platform Filipinos trust to build it right — then buy everything to build it.
